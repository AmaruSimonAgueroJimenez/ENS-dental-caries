# All-predictor importance and neural-network comparison

This is an exploratory, dataset-informed revision requested after reviewing the completed dietary-increment analysis. The emphasis returns to the earlier manuscript sequence: participant profile, comparison of models, and the importance of individual predictors. The prior dietary proposal and Andrea Correa's manuscript correction remain separately preserved. This document fixes the new neural-network search and interpretation before those fits; it does not prospectively register the study or erase knowledge of earlier results.

## Population and information sets

Use the same 5,036 dentate participants, binary prevalent caries outcome, questionnaire recodings, working examination-phase expansion weight, five PSU-disjoint outer folds and three inner folds as the completed dietary analysis. Preserve the full survey design for conditional uncertainty calculations. Confirmation of the working weight against the official examination-phase manual remains outstanding.

The main descriptive classification scenario includes all 44 available constructs: 17 context variables, 15 dietary-consumption indicators (including water and beverage volumes), six label behaviours, and six concurrent oral-status indicators. A complementary scenario excludes the six oral-status indicators and has 38 constructs. Dental visits and remaining-teeth information remain available in both scenarios; neither is a preclinical or future-incidence prediction model. Symptoms and visits may respond to existing disease. The recorded outcome, decayed-tooth counts and identifiers are excluded from predictors.

## Models and fixed new search

Reuse the existing outer held-out probabilities, tuning decisions and fold scores for spline logistic regression, random forest and histogram gradient boosting in the two scenarios. Do not change their candidate grids or select models using outer-fold performance. Add one regularized multilayer-perceptron family, evaluated separately in each scenario, for eight model variants in total.

The neural network uses scikit-learn MLPClassifier with ReLU hidden layers and Adam, batch size 128, weighted training with weights normalized to mean one, and the existing random-seed convention. Six candidates combine hidden-layer sizes (32,16) or (64,32) with three (L2 alpha, maximum-epoch) settings: (1,100), (10,100), and (10,250). Learning rate is fixed at 0.001. Select each outer fit's candidate by pooled inner held-out survey-weighted log loss. Execute the fixed training-epoch budget without internal random validation or validation-based early stopping. Record actual epochs, final training loss, warnings and finite-probability checks; reaching a fixed epoch budget is not a claim of numerical convergence. CPU execution and local software versions are recorded.

Learn numeric median imputation, missingness indicators, numeric standardization and categorical one-hot encoding only within the relevant training data. Preserve named categories and unseen-category handling. Retain the prior log1p transformation of the same four beverage frequency/volume fields and the original uncapped observations. Do not oversample, use SMOTE, balance class weights, screen predictors against the outcome or infer missing exposures. Model-specific preprocessing and bounded candidate grids are disclosed; this is not an exhaustive architecture benchmark.

## Importance and comparison

Estimate marginal raw-variable permutation importance on each model's outer held-out fold, five repeats per variable. Use a common deterministic donor-permutation convention across models. Calculate survey-weighted Brier increase and AUC decrease; positive values indicate worse performance after perturbation. Jointly permute the context, diet, label and oral-status blocks using one donor row per block to preserve within-block combinations. Reuse verified spline and forest importance outputs. To obtain missing boosting importances, reconstruct only its selected external-training fits and require reproduced held-out probabilities to match the preserved outputs within absolute tolerance 1e-12 before permuting.

Present full 44-variable results and signed estimates, with top-variable plots accompanied by complete supplementary tables. Order each model's display by its own held-out mean Brier increase. Weight fold means by held-out expansion totals; show minimum–maximum fold means as descriptive stability ranges, not confidence intervals. Cross-model summaries may describe ranks, frequency among the top ten, and the number of models with a positive mean importance. Such agreement is descriptive, not a significance test. Retain weak, zero and negative findings, including water. Correlated variables can share importance, and marginal permutation may create implausible combinations. Importance does not identify causal determinants or protective directions and is not a percentage of disease explained.

Report survey-weighted AUC, Brier score, log loss and calibration for all eight variants. Use the same 1,000 shared stratified PSU-bootstrap draws for conditional intervals and paired comparisons of MLP with each classical family within each scenario. Also compare 44 versus 38 constructs within each family, labeling the result as the contribution of concurrent oral-status information. Positive oriented gains favour the model under comparison; all comparisons are exploratory, without multiplicity-adjusted confirmatory claims. The bootstrap holds trained models, selected settings and partitions fixed and does not represent external validation or all redevelopment uncertainty.

The publication argument is identification and stability of informative questionnaire and clinical variables under a reproducible classification analysis.
